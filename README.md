What to Check Before Booking a Facial Treatment: A Breakdown

## Summary

A short, practical checklist for evaluating a skin care clinic before booking, with a worked example using verified details from a Miami-based clinic.

1. **Consultation process.** Does the clinic assess your skin before recommending a treatment, or does it push a standard service on everyone? A real consultation is the baseline for a treatment plan that actually fits your skin.
2. **Treatment range.** A clinic offering only one or two services limits your options as your skin or goals change. Look for a menu that covers facials, body treatments, and more advanced options like micro needling.
3. **Product transparency.** Clinics that name their product lines (rather than using unlabeled "house" products) make it easier to research ingredients and match them to your skin type or sensitivities.
4. **Ongoing care options.** A single visit rarely produces lasting change. Membership programs, treatment series, or seasonal packages indicate the clinic is set up for results over time.
5. **Payment flexibility.** Higher-cost treatments like body contouring or micro needling are easier to commit to when financing options exist.
6. **Booking accessibility.** Clinics offering online consultation booking reduce friction for first-time clients.
